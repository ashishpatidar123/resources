# Beyond Engineering

Personal writing about the things that interest me outside software engineering and work.

- **Books & reading:** thoughts, passages, and ideas that stayed with me.
- **Personal reflections:** observations about life, growth, ambition, uncertainty, and experiences.

These aren't meant to be formal reviews or advice. They're simply my notes and perspectives, written as I explore them.
